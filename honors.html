Scholarships & Honors

EPFL Excellence Scholarship (ESOP)

Awarded 2025

A highly competitive, merit-based fellowship awarded by EPFL to a limited number of incoming Master's students with outstanding academic records. The scholarship covers tuition fees and provides a monthly stipend to support living expenses in Lausanne, Switzerland, enabling recipients to focus entirely on their research and studies.

UAB Best Academic Record

Awarded 2025

Recognized for achieving the highest academic performance across all engineering degrees at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (UAB) during the promotion graduating in the 2024-2025 academic year.

Ibiza Best Academic Record

Awarded 2025

Awarded for achieving the highest academic performance among all students from Santa Eulària des Riu enrolled in university studies during the 2023-2024 academic year.

Best Presentation Award, Computer Vision Center

Awarded 2025

Received for the final presentation of my research internship at the CVC. This award recognizes the clarity, scientific contribution, and delivery of the presentation on "HyperNVD" among all research interns.

DEI Scholarship, CVPR 2025

Awarded 2025

A competitive travel and registration grant to attend the Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), supporting diversity, equity, and inclusion in the computer vision community.

DEI Scholarship, ECCV 2024

Awarded 2024

Selected as one of 29 outstanding researchers from a pool of 524 applicants to receive a grant supporting attendance at the European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV).

Elena Maseras Scholarship (CVC)

Awarded 2024

A scholarship from the Computer Vision Center to support promising young researchers in pursuing their scientific careers in computer vision and artificial intelligence.

Best Presentation Award, Computer Vision Center

Awarded 2024

Awarded for the presentation of my work on "Low-light image enhacement" during the CVC's annual intern showcase, recognizing the project's innovation and the quality of the presentation.

Rosa Sensat Scholarship (CVC)

Awarded 2023

An introductory research scholarship provided by the Computer Vision Center to support undergraduate students beginning their research journey.

CVC Research Scholarship

Awarded 2023

A grant provided by the Computer Vision Center to fund my initial research activities and collaboration on the "Low-light enhancement" project.
