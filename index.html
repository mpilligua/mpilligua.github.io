Maria Pilligua

I'm a Master's student in Data Science at EPFL, on an Excellence Fellowship (ESOP, top 0.004% of incoming MSc students), where I previously did research at the EPFL Computer Vision Lab on transformer architectures for 3D scene reconstruction. I'm currently interning at CARIAD (Volkswagen Group), working on perception for autonomous vehicles by fusing camera and radar information.

I graduated in Artificial Intelligence from the Autonomous University of Barcelona with the highest GPA across all engineering degrees (9.41/10). I have 5 papers at CVPR / ECCV (4 as first author), specializing in generative AI and controllability in vision models. I previously worked as a Machine Learning intern at GoodNotes in London, where I shipped a diffusion-based text-to-SVG feature now used by millions.
